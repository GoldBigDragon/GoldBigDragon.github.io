[Survival Manager] Introduction

Last updated: 2026-10-26 12:00:00

1. Background

While exploring ways to put Hongik Ingan, a foundational principle of Korean philosophy, into practice, I came up with the idea of developing a disaster preparedness application that could provide practical assistance in a wide range of emergency situations.

2. Key Features and Benefits

  • Developed for the public good, with no advertisements and no cost to use.
  • All data is stored locally on your device. The developer does not collect any user information.
  • Most features remain available offline.
  • Supports Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, and Russian.
  • The graphics are lightweight because the developer isn't much of an artist (!).

3. Features

3-1. Mental Well-being

  • Features designed to help users maintain their mental well-being in confined, dark environments where social interaction may be limited.
Feature Description
Virtual Adjutant Displays various text messages, including the current time, disaster preparedness tips, reminders about essential supplies that have not been stockpiled, and comments on members carrying overweight bags. Custom user-generated content can also be downloaded.
Survival Journal Record your experiences with titles, text, images, weather conditions, and daily mood entries.
MIDI Composer Compose music using up to 16 tracks and 128 instruments. Save your compositions in MML or MIDI format.

3-2. Knowledge

  • Features that help users acquire and review knowledge even when an internet connection is unavailable.
Feature Description
Loading Tips Displays disaster preparedness tips on the loading screen for approximately five seconds whenever the application starts.
Knowledge Base Access a variety of reference documents offline. Additional user-generated content can be downloaded when an internet connection is available.

3-3. Survival

  • Features designed to improve the chances of survival during disasters and emergencies.
Feature Description
Action Guides Contains 40 guides covering war, natural disasters, social disasters, and everyday safety. The guides are based on official government resources, disaster preparedness books, and AI-generated responses. Selected guides include illustrated, three-step quick guides to help users respond promptly in urgent situations.
Resource Dashboard View all registered food and drinking water supplies. Check how many days your current stockpile will last, monitor the condition of your resources, and identify who is carrying each item.
Off-Grid Chat Provides short-range messaging through Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), Wi-Fi Direct, and Wi-Fi LAN, allowing users to communicate and request assistance when internet access is unavailable. Messages are encrypted in transit, and chat rooms can be protected with passwords.
Offline Maps Load images stored on your device and use them as maps. When connected to the internet, download OpenStreetMap-based maps for more detailed navigation. Downloaded maps remain accessible offline. With downloaded maps, you can display your GPS-based location, place pins with various icons and text labels, measure distances, and simulate the effects of a nuclear explosion.
Compass & Level Provides compass and leveling functions using your device's built-in sensors. These features will not work on devices that lack the required sensors.
Water Purification Calculator Calculates the required dosage and waiting time for disinfecting drinking water with chlorine-based bleach or iodine tincture, based on standard guidance from the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) and the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).
Morse Code Enter Morse code manually or look up characters in a reference dictionary. Output Morse code using sound, vibration, or a camera flash. With microphone permission enabled, incoming Morse code signals can be automatically decoded.
SOS Sound Looper Repeatedly broadcasts acoustic signals to help rescuers locate people in distress.
Astronomy Tools Uses your current latitude and longitude to calculate sunrise and sunset times, determine direction using the sun, and display lunar phases and illumination information.
Emergency Radio Frequencies Browse AM/FM radio frequencies, broadcast days, and broadcast schedules for stations in various countries.
Emergency Alerts & Safety Map Requires an internet connection and opens the official safety map and emergency alert lookup services provided through National Disaster and Safety Portal (safekorea.go.kr).

3-4. Planning

  • Features that help users prepare for disasters and organize their daily activities.
Feature Description
Disaster Prep Checklists Review checklists of essential disaster preparedness supplies for bug-out bags, homes, organizations, and vehicles.
Planner Create daily, weekly, and yearly plans, manage anniversaries, and organize evacuation plans. Review your plans through daily checklists.

3-5. Organization

  • Features designed to assist administrators in organizing and managing groups during disaster situations.
Feature Description
Member & Asset Management Register detailed information about people and objects. Member profiles can serve as emergency contact records or store identifying descriptions of specific individuals. Manage items carried by registered members or stored in registered assets. Additional nutritional information can be recorded for food. For medications, you can register appearance, indications, side effects, precautions, medication type, total quantity, daily dosage, per-dose quantity, timing, administration instructions, and ingredient information. Ingredient information follows the drug classification published by the Korea Pharmaceutical Information Center (KPIC), as of November 16, 2025.
Data Backup & Sync Back up data stored on your device or transfer and synchronize it with another device.
Decision Tools Provides dice, ladder roulette, drawing lots, and anonymous voting to help groups make decisions.
Food Rationing Calculator Reviews all registered food supplies and calculates rationing recommendations based on group size, age-specific daily calorie and water requirements, nutritional content, available quantities, and remaining shelf life.
Cipher Generator Encrypt and decrypt messages using cipher algorithms so that only members who know the relevant method or key can understand them.
Triage System Provides patient triage methods to help allocate limited medical resources efficiently during mass-casualty incidents.
Quarantine Management Helps manage potential outbreaks by tracking patient symptoms and isolation periods. A contact-tracing graph visualizes potential exposure links based on patients' visit locations and times.
Ration Ticketing System Generate and verify ration tickets to support organized distribution when managing logistics for a government-run or privately organized relief operation. Tickets are issued as QR codes. If the camera is unavailable or a QR code is damaged, tickets can still be verified by entering the 64-digit serial number printed at the bottom.

3-6. Utilities

  • Additional tools designed to support disaster preparedness and everyday tasks.
Feature Description
Unit Converter Convert units for length, data size, area, volume, speed, time, pressure, energy, fuel economy, temperature, frequency, mass, plane angle, and more.
Metronome Produces beats at tempos ranging from 40 to 256 BPM using sound, vibration, or a camera flash. It can also help maintain a consistent rhythm during cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR).
PDF Export Export various types of information, including knowledge documents, action guides, plans, member information, radio frequencies, and Morse code references, to PDF files for printing and offline use.
