# 8. Wildfire

## 8-1. Getting Away
1. Work out the wind direction first, and move into the wind - away from the flames.
2. Fire runs uphill fast. Never move to ground above the fire.
3. Head for safety: ground that has already burned, low ground, roads, fields, open lots, the far side of rocks.
4. If you are camping, use roads well away from the mountain to get clear of the fire's spread.

## 8-2. If There Is No Time to Move
1. Cover your nose and mouth with clothing or a handkerchief to limit smoke.
2. Find low ground and quickly clear away leaf litter and branches - anything that burns.
3. Put the wind at your back, cover your face and any exposed skin, and lie flat and low.
4. Wait for the fire front to pass.

## 8-3. Never Do This
- Moving to ground above the flames
- Sheltering in tents, greenhouses, or other flammable structures
- Heading blindly down into a valley - fire runs up valleys even faster

# 9. Rain, Lightning, and Landslides

1. Put rain gear on immediately to prevent hypothermia.
2. In light rain you can carry on, but slow down and watch your footing.
3. In heavy rain or lightning, descend at once or take a proper shelter.
4. In lightning, avoid tall trees, peaks, and ridges - anything high - and move to lower ground. On open ground, put your feet together and crouch low.
5. Stream levels rise sharply in rain. Stay away, and if you are near a watercourse, move to high ground at once.

> Warning signs of a landslide - get out immediately
> A sudden smell of earth / the stream turning muddy all at once / a roar from the mountain.
> Other signs: new cracks or bulges on a slope, trees and utility poles leaning, and a stream suddenly running dry - which means something upstream is blocking it.
