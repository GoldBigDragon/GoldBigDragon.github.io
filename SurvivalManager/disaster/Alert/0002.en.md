# CBR Warning (Chemical, Biological, Radiological)

Issued when the release of a chemical or biological agent has been detected, or when contamination from such a weapon is expected or an attack has been confirmed. It covers both chemical and biological attacks.

> You may not know immediately which kind of attack it is. Prepare for both at once.

---

# 4. Chemical Attack

## 4-1. Protect Yourself
1. Put on a CBR gas mask - the airway comes first.
   - Chemical agents are absorbed through skin too, but the airway is far more vulnerable. Protect it before anything else.
   - With no mask, use a face mask or a wet towel or cloth.
2. Minimize exposed skin with rain gear, rubber gloves, and long clothing.
3. Get out of the contaminated area quickly.

## 4-2. Which Way to Move

| Situation | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| The wind is blowing toward the contaminated area | Move against the wind |
| The wind is blowing from the contaminated area toward you | Move perpendicular to the wind |

> Chemical agents are heavier than air and sink.
> So the safe place is a high floor indoors, sealed against outside air. Ground level and basements can hold high concentrations and are dangerous.

> If you have taken shelter underground on an air raid warning and a chemical attack is then confirmed, leave the basement at once for uncontaminated ground or a high floor indoors.

## 4-3. If There Is No Time to Get Out - Seal a Room
1. Choose an interior space that can be sealed against outside air (a bathroom, for example).
2. Close the door and windows and seal the gaps with wet towels, newspaper, or aluminum tape.
3. Switch off the air conditioning, extractor fans, and air purifiers.
4. Seal around any ventilation that connects to the outside with plastic wrap and adhesive tape.

## 4-4. Food and Water
- If you have time, seal food in airtight containers or plastic, and put the lids on water bottles and storage jars.
- Contaminants in food cause internal exposure or poisoning when eaten.

## 4-5. If You Have Been Contaminated
1. Wash under running water for at least 15 minutes, then get professional treatment at a clinic or hospital.
2. Store contaminated clothing in a sealable plastic bag or container to prevent further contamination.
3. Undress slowly and carefully so contaminants do not become airborne.

# 5. Biological Attack

Biological weapons disperse micro-organisms carrying pathogens to cause casualties. Their defining trait is that detecting the attack, identifying the pathogen, and finding a treatment all take a long time.

## 5-1. Signs That Should Make You Suspect One
- Large numbers of acute cases with similar symptoms
- A disease that does not fit the region or the season
- Animals or livestock dying en masse with no clear cause

## 5-2. What to Do
1. Avoid all contact with contaminated material and with patients.
2. Protect your airway with a gas mask, face mask, or handkerchief and leave the contaminated area quickly.
3. Go to a designated shelter or your home, stay indoors, keep yourself clean, and listen to government and local authority guidance.
4. Keep outdoor activity to an absolute minimum and do not travel to other areas.
   - People from a contaminated area who move around spread the pathogen and widen the infection.
5. Follow government guidance to get tested and receive vaccination or other treatment.

## 5-3. Daily Precautions
- Boil water before drinking.
- Cook food for at least 15 minutes.
- Keep clean, and air bedding in sunlight often.
- Livestock and pets can transmit infection too - check and manage them thoroughly.
- Follow government broadcasts, health authorities, and civil defense personnel to prevent further infection.

## 5-4. Suspicious Mail and Powders
- Do not open suspicious mail. Store it somewhere isolated and report it promptly to your local health center or the Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency (1339).
- If a suspicious liquid or powder spills out, cover it with plastic or wrapping to stop it spreading.

---

# What Not to Do During a CBR Warning
- Running ventilation equipment (air conditioning, extractor fans, air purifiers)
- Eating anything in a contaminated area
- Drinking or eating suspect water or food without purifying or cooking it
- Removing your gas mask or other protective gear before the government says so
- Touching contaminated material or patients
