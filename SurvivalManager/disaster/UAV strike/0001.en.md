# 1. Why Drones Are Not Like Conventional Air Attack

- In 2025 alone, short-range combat drones killed 577 civilians and injured more than 3,000 in Ukraine.
- A drone tracks and targets one individual civilian. That is fundamentally different from artillery, which strikes an area indiscriminately.
- It flies between buildings, turns corners, and comes in through an open window.

> Conventional air-raid procedure alone will not protect you from drones. You have to identify the type and act accordingly.

# 2. The Four Types of Drone

## 2-1. Long-Range One-Way Attack Drone - Shahed / Geran-2
- An Iranian design with a delta wing about 2.5 m across; the Russian designation is Geran-2.
- Flies a pre-programmed GPS route at about 185 km/h.
- Carries a 30–50 kg high-explosive warhead.

| Damage radius | Distance |
| --- | --- |
| Structural destruction | about 19 m |
| Vehicle destruction | about 32 m |
| Lethal fragmentation | over 50 m |
| Secondary damage (glass fragments) | over 100 m |

> Sound: a motorcycle or a chainsaw. Audible from 1–3 km away, so you get several minutes of warning.

## 2-2. FPV One-Way Attack Drone
- A small quadcopter flown by a pilot watching live camera video.
- 100–120 km/h, a 1–7 kg warhead, and a lethal radius of 5–15 m.
- Flies through tight spaces, turns corners, and enters buildings through open windows.

> Sound: a high-pitched buzz, like an angry swarm of bees. Detectable at 200–500 m in a quiet setting, but in the final dive you have only 3 to 7 seconds.

## 2-3. Reconnaissance and Surveillance Drone
- Orlan-10, ZALA, DJI Mavic types. They do not strike you - they find you, and call in artillery, missiles, or a strike drone.
- Per Ukrainian military TTP, some reconnaissance drones carry signals-intelligence equipment that calculates position from mobile, radio, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth emissions.

> Sound: a relatively quiet high tone. Audible at 50–150 m in quiet conditions, and only 20–50 m in a city.

## 2-4. Bomb-Dropping Quadcopter
- Modified DJI and Autel platforms hover overhead and release grenades or antipersonnel mines.

# 3. How to Detect a Drone

## 3-1. Sound - Your Most Important Tool

> Sound is the single most important early-warning method available to a civilian.

Ukrainian military TTP states that "drones are hardest to detect at 100–300 m altitude in daylight, and you will most likely hear one before you see it."

| What you hear | What it is | Time left, and what to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Motorcycle or chainsaw | Shahed-type long-range drone | Several minutes - find a shelter now |
| High buzz like angry bees | FPV attack drone | Seconds - act immediately |
| Quiet high tone | Reconnaissance drone | You are being watched - reduce electronic signals and visual exposure |

- Keep your car windows open while driving. If road noise masks the drone, you lose the warning.

## 3-2. Sight
- Scan the sky regularly. Drones are easiest to spot at dawn and dusk.
- The rule from Ukrainian TTP: "Treat every object in the air as hostile until identified."

## 3-3. The Limits of Electronic Warning
- Strategic drones - Shaheds, cruise missiles - can be warned of early by tracking systems.
- But no effective civilian warning system currently exists for short-range FPV drones.

> That makes constant listening the only means of responding to an FPV drone.
