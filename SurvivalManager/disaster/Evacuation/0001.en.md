# 1. Know Your Shelter Beforehand

Evacuation comes suddenly. Find the nearest shelter to the places you spend time - home, work, school - before you need it.

## 1-1. Where to Shelter, by Type of Attack

| Type of attack | Where to shelter |
| --- | --- |
| Conventional weapons | Civil defense shelters, underground parking of apartment blocks and large buildings, subway stations |
| Chemical | A high floor indoors, sealed against outside air |
| Biological | A designated shelter or your home - any interior space you can seal and stay in |
| Nuclear / radiological | Civil defense shelters, underground parking of apartment blocks and large buildings, subway stations |

## 1-2. How to Find One
Search "민방위 대피소" (civil defense shelter) in [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/민방위%20대피소), [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/), or the [Safety Dideemdol app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.go.nema.disasteralert_new) to see the nearest one to where you are.

> Go and look at it once, in ordinary times. Knowing where the entrance is, whether supplies are stocked, and how long it takes to get there is a completely different thing from not knowing.

## 1-3. Two Exceptions
- During the monsoon and typhoon season, when heavy rain is expected, avoid underground facilities in flood-prone low-lying areas and go somewhere safer.
- For a chemical attack it is a high floor, not a basement. Chemical agents are heavier than air and pool underground.

# 2. The Emergency Pack

## 2-1. Principles
- The pack holds the minimum you need to live after evacuating. When evacuation comes suddenly, there is no time to gather anything.
- Prepare one per family member, and do not load all the important items into one person's pack.
- Keep it under 10% of your body weight. Heavy packs slow you down and burn energy fast.
- Anything that must stay dry - radio, documents, cash, clothes, power bank - goes in a zip-lock bag.
- A hiking pack with a hip belt and chest strap is far less tiring.
- Choose food that needs no cooking, and check and rotate expiry dates regularly.
- Keep plenty of spare batteries. You will be listening to government broadcasts on the radio.
- Do not keep it only at home. You may not be home when it happens - keep a simple pack at work or school too, and water and food in the car.

## 2-2. Contents
Two to three days of water, food, essentials, medicines, a radio, a flashlight, and CBR protective items.

| Category | Items |
| :---: | :--- |
| Food and water | 4 × 500 mL bottled water, emergency food, water filter, purification tablets, salt, multivitamins |
| Infants and elderly | Formula, baby food, bottles, a small toy, retort porridge |
| Clothing | Gas mask, protective suit, disposable rain poncho, gloves, socks, safety glasses, face masks, underwear, hat, windproof jacket |
| Medical | Antibiotic ointment, anti-diarrheal, antipyretic, painkillers, antacids, bandages, antiseptic, medications, prescriptions for chronic conditions |
| Warmth | Emergency foil blanket, hand warmers |
| Light | Flashlight, headlamp, batteries |
| Hygiene | Soap, tissue, towel, sanitary pads, toothbrush, toothpaste, wet wipes |
| Tools | Radio, whistle, lighter, multi-tool knife, scissors, nail clippers, screwdriver, tweezers, plastic bags, zip-lock bags, tape, pen and paper, compass, a map of your neighborhood, power bank, USB cable, charger, utensils, disposable plates and cups, cash |
| Documents | Copy of your ID, important documents, contacts for family and friends |

## 2-3. Important Documents
- Keep copies of your bankbook, insurance policy, contracts, and ID in the pack, in case the originals are lost.
- Put them in a waterproof zip-lock bag.

# 3. The Communication Plan

## 3-1. Where to Meet
- In an emergency, communications are likely to fail and you will lose contact. Agree in advance.
- Choose somewhere you normally meet, or a safe shelter.
- Set a second and third location in case you cannot reach the first.
- Also agree on a joining point for when home is unreachable and for evacuating from school or work.

## 3-2. The Emergency Contact Card
> If your phone breaks or the battery dies, you cannot look up a single number.
> A paper contact list goes in every family member's pack.

What to write: personal details · family details · blood type · medications · phone numbers · government agency and relief organization numbers

## 3-3. Against Loss of Communications
- Install a messenger other than KakaoTalk (Telegram, Discord) on every family member's phone in advance.
- Download an offline map app such as [MAPS.ME](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mapswithme.maps.pro) so you can check evacuation routes with no signal.
