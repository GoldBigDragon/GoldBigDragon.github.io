# Prevention

## 4. How to Report
- If you suspect terrorism, report it immediately. National Intelligence Service 111 / National Police 112
- Be specific: the exact location, what you suspect or the damage you see, and the atmosphere at the scene.
- If you receive a threatening call, remember or record it in detail before reporting.

## 5. In Crowded Public Places
- Shopping malls, airports, train stations and other busy facilities are easy targets. Stay aware.
- If you see suspicious behavior or something simply feels wrong, leave quickly and report it.
- Note where the emergency exits and shelters are as a matter of habit.

## 6. Travelling Abroad

### Before You Go
- Check the travel alert level for your destination on the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs overseas travel safety site](https://www.0404.go.kr/).
- Keep an eye on the ministry's safety roaming text messages.
- Look through the crisis-by-crisis response manuals in the [Overseas Safe Travel app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.go.mofa.safetravel&hl=ko).
- Memorize a few phrases in the local language that attract attention - "Fire!", "Help me!"
- Carry a personal safety device or a whistle.

### The Four Travel Alert Levels

| Level | Color | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Blue | Exercise caution |
| 2 | Yellow | Reconsider travel |
| 3 | Red | Departure advised - come home unless your business is urgent; cancel or postpone travel |
| 4 | Black | Travel prohibited - anyone there should evacuate immediately |

※ A separate special travel advisory carries the weight of level 2 or 3.

### On the Ground - Strangers
- Be wary of anyone who suddenly starts talking to you and splits your attention.
- Refuse food or drink offered by strangers. It may contain sedatives or drugs.
- Avoid venues that tout for customers, and do not share a table with strangers.
- Never agree to go somewhere secluded - behind a building, down an alley.

### On the Ground - Moving Around
- Never hold or carry someone else's luggage. It could be drugs or a bomb.
- Do not fall asleep in a taxi or rental car. Keep the windows up and the doors locked.
- Be wary of a stranger's help when you are parked or your car has broken down.

### If You Encounter an Attack
- Move quickly against the flow of the crowd or into a nearby safe facility.
- If you cannot get away immediately, hide out of the attacker's line of sight.
- Contact the Korean mission with jurisdiction to report your situation and get safety information.
