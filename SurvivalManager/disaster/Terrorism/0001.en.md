# The Core Principle

1. RUN - leave the scene immediately, as far as you can.
2. HIDE - once you are far enough away, get out of the attacker's line of sight and secure yourself.
3. TELL - once you are safe, report it. National Intelligence Service 111 / National Police 112

| Type | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Run immediately | Mail and parcels, active shooter, explosives, vehicle ramming, drones, crowded venues, chemical and biological, radiological |
| Wait calmly for rescue | Detention, kidnapping, aircraft hijacking - here survival comes from compliance and memory, not resistance |

> False reports are punishable. Genuine reports, on the other hand, can earn a reward of up to 100 million won.

## Is Korea Safe from Terrorism?
- Cases of people being caught for supporting terrorist organizations in Korea have risen steadily, and hate-motivated attacks continue abroad - the threat directed at Korea is growing.
- New forms of attack using drones, vehicle ramming, and mail or parcels can happen at any time.
- Terrorism does not discriminate by nationality, race, or region. There is no safe zone.

## The Four Terror Alert Levels

| Level | State | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attention | Low probability of an attack | Intelligence on threats to Korea received; frequent international attacks |
| Caution | Could develop into an attack | Threat intelligence becomes specific; attempted infiltration by individuals linked to international terrorist organizations |
| Alert | High probability of an attack | A terrorist organization names Korea directly in a threat; large-scale means of attack detected |
| Severe | An attack is virtually certain | Unambiguous threat intelligence; an attempt or an attack within the country |

# Response by Type of Attack

## 1. Active Shooter
- Move away along the wall, in the opposite direction from the gunfire.
- Hide out of the attacker's line of sight and block the entrance with furniture.
- If confrontation becomes unavoidable, work together with the people around you and fight back using whatever objects are at hand.

> Never come out of hiding to attack first.

## 2. Explosives
If you find a suspicious object or vehicle
- Never touch it. Get away quickly, then report it.
- Use the emergency stairs on the opposite side from the device to get out of the building. Do not use the elevator.

If you hear a blast, or immediately after one
1. Get flat on the floor at once.
2. Bring your arms and elbows together to protect your chest.
3. Cover your ears and head with your hands to protect your skull.
4. A secondary blast may follow, so stay down a while longer, then move away quickly in the direction opposite the blast.

## 3. Vehicle Ramming
- Move perpendicular to the vehicle's direction of travel, get clear of the area, and report it.
- If you cannot get away quickly, hide behind cover that blocks their line of sight.
- The attacker may leave the vehicle and carry out a second attack. Stay hidden.

## 4. Chemical and Biological
These symptoms point to a chemical or biological attack
Stinging eyes or skin · dizziness · vomiting · coughing · difficulty breathing

1. Put on a gas mask, or cover your nose and mouth with a handkerchief or tissue and breathe through it.
2. Identify the contaminated area and the source, then get out of your current position quickly.
   - If a broadcast or emergency message gives instructions, go to the designated shelter.
3. If you have been exposed, call 119 and get to a medical facility quickly.

## 5. Mail and Parcels
Mail that should make you suspicious
- Foreign mail from an unknown sender
- Excessively wrapped with tape or string
- Unusually heavy for its size, or smelling of chemicals

1. Never open it.
2. Cover it with a wastebasket, box, or blanket, seal off the area, and leave immediately.
3. Get yourself and the people nearby out, then report it.

## 6. Drones
- If you see a drone you suspect is being used as a weapon, take shelter inside a building or a car, then report it.
- If a suspicious liquid or gas is being sprayed, cover your nose and mouth with a handkerchief or mask and move against the wind or perpendicular to it.
- If you suspect it is carrying explosives, prepare for an airburst - get to a nearby structure or building, or cover your head and chest and lie flat.

## 7. Crowded Venues
- Move in the direction away from where the attack appears to have happened.
- In an enclosed space, use the emergency exit you identified in advance.
- If you cannot get out, find somewhere to hide.
- Do not mix into the crowd. Places where people gather are easy targets - move against the flow of the crowd or into a nearby safe facility.

## 8. Radiological
- If a nuclear facility is attacked or radioactive material is used, take shelter inside the nearest building.
  - Not a building made unsafe by collapse or fire.
- Check official announcements on TV, radio, or the internet and follow instructions.
- If you were in a contaminated area or in contact with someone possibly contaminated, call the radiation emergency medical facility (02-3399-5959).

## 9. Detention, Kidnapping, Hijacking
> This is the one category where the answer is compliance, not resistance.

- Comply with the captors' instructions, calmly and carefully.
- If spoken to, answer quietly and avoid drawing attention to yourself.
- Do not provoke them, and cooperate naturally if asked to write a ransom letter or make a recording.
- When blindfolded, memorize everything you can - sounds, smells, the captors' voices, road conditions while moving. These become decisive leads for the investigation.
- Hold on to the expectation of rescue and keep your health up.
- During a rescue operation, get flat on the floor. It also lets the rescue team tell hostages from attackers.
- Ministry of Foreign Affairs Consular Call Center (24/7): from Korea (02)3210-0404 / from abroad +82-2-3210-0404
