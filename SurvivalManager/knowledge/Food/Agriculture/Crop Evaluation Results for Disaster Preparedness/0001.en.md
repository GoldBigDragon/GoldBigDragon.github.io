## Introduction
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This is the result of scoring 222 crops on 25 items. The scoring criteria and their basis are in the Crop Selection Criteria for Disaster Preparedness document, and how to grow each crop is in the individual crop documents.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**A grade is not an absolute score but a relative position among the 222 crops.** SS means the top 2%, S the top 7%, and A the top 18%. When a crop is added, the corresponding scores are updated, but the percentile definitions do not change.

> **Do not take this ranking as the order in which to plant.**
> Many of the top crops are wild or semi-wild species with no official yield statistics, and their domestic spread is low, so **in many cases seed cannot be obtained on the day of the disaster.** The list you can actually use emerges only when this is read together with the spread item.
