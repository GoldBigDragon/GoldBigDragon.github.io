## Introduction
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This is the result of scoring 222 crop species on 25 criteria. The scoring criteria and their basis are in the **[Crop Selection Criteria]** document; how to actually grow each crop is in the **[Cultivation Guides]** document.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**A grade is not an absolute score but a relative position among the 222 species.** SS means the top 2%, S the top 7%, and A the top 18%. If crops are added, the corresponding score is updated, but the percentile definition does not change.

> **This ranking must not be taken as-is for the order in which to plant.**
> Many of the top crops are wild or semi-wild species with no official yield statistics, and their low prevalence in South Korea often means **you cannot obtain seed on the day disaster strikes.** Read this together with the prevalence criterion to get a list you can actually use.
