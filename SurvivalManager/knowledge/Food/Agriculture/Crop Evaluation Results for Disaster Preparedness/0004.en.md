### 4-1. Distribution of Exclusion Reasons

| Reason for exclusion | Species | Nature |
|:---|--:|:---|
| Calories per area E (below 15% of the self-sufficiency line) | 34 | Mostly wild greens, sprouts, and spice vegetables. **Top-class as supplementary food but cannot become a staple** |
| Storability E + perenniality below A | 5 | 3 mushroom species + spinach + cucumber. **Stockpiling is impossible** |

> **This does not mean discarding the excluded crops.**  
> Dandelion, godeulppaegi, Korean angelica tree shoots, and mountain garlic are useful as supplementary food. It is, however, a warning: **"do not try to fill your calories with these."** Choose the main crops of the field from the valid crops, and the excluded crops can play the role of adding nutrition alongside them.

> **The exclusion of mushrooms is resolved through the storage method.**  
> The exclusion reason for lion's mane, button mushroom, and enoki is the storability of the fresh product. **Once dried, the storability grade changes.** Note that the exclusion list is based on "unprocessed fresh" products.
