### 4-1. Distribution of Exclusion Reasons

| Exclusion reason | Species | Notes |
|:---|--:|:---|
| Calories per area E (below 15% of the self-sufficiency line) | 34 | Mostly wild greens, sprouts, and herbs. **Excellent as a supplementary food, but cannot serve as a staple.** |
| Storability E + Perenniality below A | 5 | 3 mushroom species plus spinach and cucumber. **Cannot be stockpiled.** |

> **This does not mean you should discard the excluded crops.**  
> Dandelion, godeulbbaegi, aralia shoots, and mountain garlic are useful as supplementary food. It is simply a warning that **"you should not try to fill your calorie needs with these."** Choose your field's main crops from the valid crops, and let the excluded crops play a supporting role alongside them, adding nutrition.

> **A mushroom's exclusion can be resolved by how it is stored.**  
> The reason lion's mane, button mushroom, and enoki are excluded is the storability of the raw product. **Drying them changes the storability grade.** Remember that the exclusion list is based on "raw, unprocessed" product.
