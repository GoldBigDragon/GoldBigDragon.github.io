## Introduction
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This document is an evaluation scale for deciding **which crops to plant** in a disaster. It scores crops on 25 items and assigns grades for three scenarios: short-term, long-term, and supplementary food.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The scale assumes that **chemical fertilizer, pesticides, irrigation, electricity, and fuel have all been cut off**. The conclusions differ greatly from ordinary crop-selection criteria. The crops that yield the most under conventional cultivation can lose 80% of their yield under no-input conditions, while wild tubers that nobody plants can exceed the self-sufficiency line.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The results for the 222 crops already scored are in the Crop Evaluation Results for Disaster Preparedness document, and how to grow each crop is in the individual crop documents. This document explains the basis on which the grades were assigned, and it also serves as the criteria table for evaluating a crop that is not on the list. The reason behind each definition is given along with it, so use the thresholds with an understanding of where they came from rather than simply memorizing them.
