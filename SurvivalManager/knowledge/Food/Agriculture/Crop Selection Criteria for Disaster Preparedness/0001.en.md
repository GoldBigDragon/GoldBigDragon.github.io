## Introduction
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This document is an evaluation scale for deciding **which crops to plant** in a disaster situation. It scores crops on 25 criteria and produces grades for three scenarios: short-term, long-term, and supplementary.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The scale assumes conditions in which **chemical fertilizer, pesticides, irrigation, electricity, and fuel have all been cut off.** The conclusions differ sharply from ordinary crop-selection standards. A crop that dominates conventional farming can lose 80% of its yield under zero-input conditions, while a wild tuber that no one plants can clear the self-sufficiency line.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The results already scored for 222 species are in the **[Crop Evaluation Results]** document, and how to actually grow each crop is in the **[Cultivation Guides]** document. This document explains **on what basis** those grades were assigned, and it also serves as the reference table for scoring a crop that is not on the list yourself.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;For each evaluation criterion, this document also records why it was defined that way. Knowing **why a threshold is what it is** helps far more with real judgment calls than simply memorizing the threshold.
