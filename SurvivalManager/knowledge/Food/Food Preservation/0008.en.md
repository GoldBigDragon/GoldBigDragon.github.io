## 7. Inventory Management
---
### 7-1. Managing Preservation Resources
| Resource | Method |
| :---: | :--- |
| Salt | Must always be stored in a sealed container to keep moisture out. |
| Sugar | Store in an airtight container to block moisture and pests. |

---
### 7-2. Rotating Consumption
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;No matter how well packaged, food can spoil over time, so stockpiled food must be rotated. Follow the **First In First Out** principle: consume the oldest food first and the new food after.

---
### 7-3. Checking Preservation Condition
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Even long-term stored food should be checked at intervals of 1-3 years for the items below and repackaged if necessary.

| Step | Item to check |
| :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Whether the packaging is damaged (tiny pinholes and the like) |
| 2 | Whether there is condensation, mold, or off-odor |
| 3 | Whether the oxygen absorber is saturated (replace the oxygen absorber and reseal if necessary) |
| 4 | Whether there are changes in the food's color, smell, or texture |

---
### 7-4. Checking Stockpile Quantity
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In preparation for an emergency, set as the basic goal stockpiling at least 3 days to 2 weeks of food.
