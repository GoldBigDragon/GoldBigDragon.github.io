## 7. Inventory Management
---
### 7-1. Preservation Resource Management
| Resource | Methods |
| :---: | :--- |
| Salt | Must be stored in a sealed container to prevent moisture influx. |
| Sugar | Store in an airtight container to block moisture and pests. |

---
### 7-2. Rotational Consumption
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Even the most securely packaged food supplies can deteriorate over time; therefore, stockpiled food must undergo rotational consumption. Adhere to the **First In, First Out (FIFO)** principle by consuming the oldest provisions first and placing newly acquired food at the back.

---
### 7-3. Preservation Condition Inspection
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Even for long-term stockpiled food, inspect the following items on a 1-to-3-year cycle and repackage them if necessary.

| Step | Inspection Items |
| :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Package damage (such as microscopic pinholes, etc.) |
| 2 | Presence of condensation, mold, or off-odors |
| 3 | Saturation status of oxygen absorbers (replace oxygen absorbers and reseal if necessary) |
| 4 | Changes in the color, smell, or texture of the food |

---
### 7-4. Stockpile Volume Verification
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Establish a baseline target of stockpiling at least a 3-day to 2-week supply of food to prepare for emergency situations.
