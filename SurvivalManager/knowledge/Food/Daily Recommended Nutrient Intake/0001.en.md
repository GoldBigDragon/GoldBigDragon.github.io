# Daily Recommended Nutrient Intake
---
## 1. Macronutrients
| Nutrient | Adults recommended | Deficiency Effects | Excess Effects |
|:--:|--:|--|--|
| Carbohydrates | 45–75% of total energy | Fatigue, low energy | Weight gain, hyperglycemia |
| Dietary Fiber | ≥ 25 g | Digestive impairment | Gas, bloating |
| Protein | 0.83 g/kg | Muscle loss, immune decline | Renal burden |
| Total Fat | ≤ 30% of energy | Essential FA deficiency | Obesity, CVD |
| Saturated Fat | ≤ 10% of energy | – | Increased LDL, CVD risk |
| Trans Fat | < 1% of energy | – | Increased CVD risk |
| Free Sugars | < 10% of energy | – | Dental caries, obesity, diabetes |

Source: [WHO Guidelines on fats and carbohydrates (2023)](https://www.who.int/news/item/17-07-2023-who-updates-guidelines-on-fats-and-carbohydrates)
