# Daily Recommended Nutrient Intake
---
## 1. Macronutrients
| Nutrient | Recommended daily intake (adults) | Deficiency | Excess |
|:--:|--:|--|--|
| Carbohydrates | 45-75% of total energy | Energy shortage, fatigue | Weight gain, rise in blood sugar |
| Dietary fiber | 25 g or more | Reduced digestive function | Gas, abdominal bloating |
| Protein | 0.83 g/kg/day | Muscle loss, weakened immunity | Increased burden on the kidneys |
| Fat (total) | 30% or less of total energy | Essential fatty acid shortage | Obesity, cardiovascular disease |
| Saturated fat | 10% or less of total energy | – | Rise in LDL cholesterol |
| Trans fat | Less than 1% of total energy | – | Increased risk of cardiovascular disease |
| Sugars | Less than 10% of total energy | – | Risk of tooth decay, obesity, diabetes |

Source: [WHO Guidelines on fats and carbohydrates (2023)](https://www.who.int/news/item/17-07-2023-who-updates-guidelines-on-fats-and-carbohydrates)
