# 3. Humanity
---
## 3-1. Understanding the Situation
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In a disaster situation, you can't predict exactly what you'll have to do to survive, but you must be prepared for any situation. Can you kill someone quickly without hesitation, using a knife or your bare hands? Is it really possible? In my opinion, you can't be sure unless you're in a situation where you must kill or be killed.  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I've seen a man holding a gun who couldn't fire a single shot before being stabbed to death by an attacker with a knife. He either lacked the courage to shoot or froze in that moment.  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The first week of accepting a disaster is the hardest, but eventually, you will accept the new reality. It will be in your best interest to do so. I have seen people scream and panic when the situation started. They seemed unwilling to even acknowledge the reality. At the same time, others were gathering weapons from an abandoned police station. It's obvious which group is better suited for survival.  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;If you don't accept the disaster, you will find it hard to return to normal life even after everything is over. Remember that.  

---

## 3-2. The Gray People
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;There were 10 to 15 armed robbers, and sometimes up to 50, but with them were ordinary people as well. Fathers, grandfathers, ordinary people were stealing and killing. It was a situation where good and evil were not clearly distinguishable, and most people were ready to do anything.  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When the situation collapsed, someone would definitely do bad things to save a child. They wouldn't be doing it to become a hero, but simply to survive with their family.  

---

## 3-3. Mercy Breeds Greed
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Charity does not work. Those who helped others or shared food ended up either dying or being beaten and having everything taken from them. If you show kindness, you will eventually lose everything.  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;We simply sent away reckless people who tried to steal. However, we showed no mercy to those who attacked our homes to take everything or threatened our families. If you let those people go or believe their promises, they will come back next time with more people.
