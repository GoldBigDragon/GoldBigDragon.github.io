# 2. Group
---
## 2-1. Formation of a Group
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In a disaster situation, everything changes. The worst people become the important figures, and they monopolize resources, markets, and weapons. Therefore, when a disaster strikes in a city, you must form a group with your family, friends, and trusted people as quickly as possible and organize it systematically. (It is not recommended to elect a group leader democratically.)  

---

## 2-2. Group Members
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;My group was composed only of my family. I had a few close friends around, but my best friend was my family. One thing is certain, most groups without strong bonds between members eventually turned into bandit groups.  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Most of the time, I couldn’t distinguish between enemies and friends. Everyone except my family was a potential enemy. I never included strangers in my group. If a friend had to choose between their child and my death, who would they choose?  

---

## 2-3. Street Community
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When the disaster struck, there was no organized military to protect us, and we had to take up arms ourselves to protect us from the enemy and bandits. To protect ourselves, we formed street communities separate from the group.  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The street I lived on formed patrols to watch for armed bandits and enemies, and five armed people would patrol the streets every night. Despite this, gunfights frequently broke out in the streets, and we sometimes used vehicles to set up barricades.  

---

## 2-4. Surviving Alone is Almost Impossible
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In my personal opinion, surviving alone is almost impossible. No matter how well prepared and armed you are, I’ve seen many people die in such situations. I believe it’s best to group together with your family or very close friends, acquiring multiple skills and preparing as much as possible.  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;If I bring family or really (really really really) good friends into my group, I don’t think it just increases the amount of food consumed. Instead, I see it as an opportunity to secure more weapons and power. Think simply. During the first period, all the weak people disappeared, and the remaining groups had to fight each other.  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Small family units or people living alone find it hard to survive in urban disaster situations. No matter how many supplies you stock up and hide in your house, eventually armed bandits will come, and you’ll only be able to defend with a couple of guns. When armed bandits storm in, you need many weapons and people to use them. That’s why families with smaller households joined larger families to live together in bigger houses.  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Relief supplies occasionally fell from the sky, but you could never be sure when you’d receive them, and even if supplies came, there was no control over them. To secure relief supplies, you must remember that it is not **[first come, first served]**, but **[the more armed you are, the more you get]**.
