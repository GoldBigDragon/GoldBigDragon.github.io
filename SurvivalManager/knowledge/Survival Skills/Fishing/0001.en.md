## Introduction
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When electricity and supply chains are cut off, the first thing people lose is not calories but protein and fat. Grain and roots can be stored, but any stored food eventually runs out. From that point on, you have to take what the surrounding environment produces on its own, and aquatic animals gather at a far higher density than land animals. Comparing equal areas of land and water, the animal biomass per unit area is generally more than an order of magnitude greater on the water side. Because fish do not spend energy maintaining body temperature, they convert what they eat into flesh more efficiently than land-dwelling warm-blooded animals, which is why the same ecosystem can support far more individuals in the water.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The decisive advantage of fishing is that the act of catching is separated from human labor. Hunting only works while a person is moving, but traps, gillnets, and longlines keep working even while people sleep at night. For someone who is starving, this is not a mere convenience but a condition for survival. Once you are hungry, your daily calorie budget itself shrinks, and if you burn that budget chasing and searching for prey, your return cannot outpace what you spend, and you weaken even faster as a result. Setting up gear and waiting is almost the only way to reverse this calorie balance.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This document sets out the principles for building fishing gear and where to place it, and why a caught fish spoils so quickly and which species are dangerous and why. In ordinary times, however, this knowledge cannot be put into practice as it stands. In South Korea, fishing in inland waters and coastal areas is governed by the Fisheries Act, the Inland Water Fisheries Act, and the Fisheries Resources Management Act, and the law sets the types of gear allowed, mesh size, the minimum length below which catching a fish is prohibited, and closed seasons. Using nets or traps without a permit, installing an eosal (fixed fish weir), or catching individuals below the set minimum length are all punishable offenses. Most other countries have similarly intentioned regulations. This document is a compilation of knowledge premised on a disaster situation in which public institutions are no longer functioning; in ordinary times, you must check your own country's fisheries-related laws and act only within them.
