## Introduction
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When electricity and logistics are cut off, what people lose first is not calories but protein and fat. Stored food will run out someday, and after that you have to take what the environment produces by itself. Per unit area, the biomass of aquatic animals is generally an order of magnitude or more greater than that of land animals. Fish do not spend calories on maintaining body temperature, so their efficiency in turning food into flesh is also higher than that of land warm-blooded animals.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The decisive advantage of fishing is that the act of catching is separated from human labor. Fish traps, gill nets, and longlines work even at night while people sleep. When you are starving, the daily calorie budget you can spend itself shrinks, and if you burn calories on chasing and searching, the amount recovered does not exceed the amount invested and you weaken faster. Building gear and waiting is almost the only way to reverse this calorie balance.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This document covers the principles and placement of making gear, why caught fish spoil quickly, and dangerous species.

> **Caution:** In ordinary times this knowledge cannot be carried out as is. In South Korea, fishing in inland waters and coastal areas is governed by the Fisheries Act, the Inland Water Fisheries Act, and the Fishery Resources Management Act, and gear types, mesh sizes, minimum legal lengths, and closed seasons are set by law. Using nets or traps without a permit, setting up weirs, and catching individuals below the set length are punishable. Most other countries have regulations of the same intent. This document assumes a disaster situation in which public structures do not function; in ordinary times, check the fishing laws of your country and act only within them.
