# 3. Bends
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A knot-tying method used to join two separate lines together.  

---
## 3-1. Fisherman's Knot
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Used to connect two ropes of equal diameter.  

1. Position the two lines parallel to each other.  
![image](./media/Fisherman's_Knot_001.png)  
2. Tie an overhand knot with the upper line around the lower line.  
![image](./media/Fisherman's_Knot_002.png)  
3. Tie an overhand knot with the lower line around the upper line.  
![image](./media/Fisherman's_Knot_003.png)  
4. Pull both standing parts simultaneously to slide and lock the two knots together.  
![image](./media/Fisherman's_Knot_004.png)  
5. The completed Fisherman's Knot.  
![image](./media/Fisherman's_Knot_005.png)  

---
## 3-2. Double Simon (Under)
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The most secure variation of the Simple Simon knot, ideal for joining slippery synthetic lines or lines of different diameters.  

1. Form a bight with the upper line, insert the lower line through the bight, and direct it to the right.  
![image](./media/Double_Simple_Simon_Under_001.png)  
2. Wrap the lower line around the upper bight, moving upward.  
![image](./media/Double_Simple_Simon_Under_002.png)  
3. Wrap the lower line around the upper bight moving downward, ensuring it passes beneath the lower-right section of the loop.  
![image](./media/Double_Simple_Simon_Under_003.png)  
4. Pass the lower line from the inside to the outside of the upper bight and pull to secure.  
![image](./media/Double_Simple_Simon_Under_004.png)  
5. The completed Double Simon (Under) knot.  
![image](./media/Double_Simple_Simon_Under_005.png)  

---
## 3-3. Alberto Knot
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commonly used in fishing to connect two lines of different diameters, such as a heavy leader and a thin mainline.  

1. Form a loop with the thicker lower line, insert the thinner upper line through the loop, and wrap it down around both strands of the lower line multiple times.  
![image](./media/Alberto_Knot_001.png)  
2. Wrap the upper line back up over the previous wraps multiple times, pass it back through the lower loop, and pull to tighten.  
![image](./media/Alberto_Knot_002.png)  
3. The completed Alberto Knot.  
![image](./media/Alberto_Knot_003.png)
