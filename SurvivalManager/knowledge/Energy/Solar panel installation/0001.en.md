## Introduction
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This document is a crash course guide designed to help individuals with absolutely no prior knowledge of electricity to independently install and utilize a 24V solar power system during disaster scenarios. It minimizes theoretical concepts and focuses exclusively on the practical knowledge necessary to set up a solar generator.  
  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Because the subsequent sections are built upon the concepts introduced in the preceding chapters, it is highly recommended to read sequentially starting from [1. Basics of Electricity] if this is your first time installing a solar power system. It is best to proceed with all tasks slowly, taking it one step at a time.  
  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**This guide provides fundamental knowledge for emergency use. Whenever circumstances permit, you must seek assistance from an experienced individual.**
