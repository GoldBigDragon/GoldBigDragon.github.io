## 4. Wind, Steam, Wood Gas, and Human Power
### 4-1. Wind Power
> **Wind power is proportional to the cube of wind speed. If the wind speed is halved, the power output drops to 12.5%; if the wind speed doubles, the power output increases eightfold.**

| Wind Speed | Relative Output |
|---:|---:|
| 3 m/s | 1 |
| 4 m/s | 2.4 |
| 5 m/s | 4.6 |
| 6 m/s | 8 |
| 8 m/s | 19 |
| 10 m/s | 37 |

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The reason this relationship makes wind power difficult to handle is that in places with weak wind, no matter how large the blades are, it is of no use. In places where the annual average wind speed is less than 4 m/s, the effort invested in building wind power facilities is rarely recovered. Most of inland Korea falls into this category, and meaningful wind can only be obtained along coasts, ridge lines, and open high ground.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Another factor determining output is the area swept by the blades, which is proportional to the square of the diameter. However, doubling the diameter also increases the force, weight, and vibration acting on the blades, making the structure unable to withstand it. Therefore, the most effective real-world improvement is raising the tower height. Because friction near the ground significantly reduces wind speed, raising the tower to more than twice the height of surrounding obstacles alone increases output several times over.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Safety braking systems are not optional. When rotation speed increases during strong winds, centrifugal force increases as the square of the RPM, causing blades to detach from the hub. Methods such as furling the tail vane to receive wind at an angle, changing blade pitch angles, or shorting generator outputs for electrical braking are commonly used. Because the moment the battery becomes full and the load disappears is the most dangerous, a dump load is essential.

### 4-2. A Steam Engine Is All About the Boiler
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The operating principle of a steam engine relies on a single fact: when water turns into steam, its volume expands roughly 1,700 times. Preventing this expansion inside a sealed vessel creates pressure, which then pushes a piston or turns turbine blades.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This exact same fact is the root of danger. When a pressurized boiler ruptures, the hot water inside instantly converts entirely into steam and explodes. This is a completely different phenomenon from compressed air leaking, producing enough energy from even a small boiler to collapse a building.

> **A boiler without a safety valve must never be built. Unless there is a device that opens automatically to release steam when pressure exceeds a set value, that boiler is a bomb waiting to explode at any moment.**

| Device | Function |
|:---|:---|
| Safety Valve | Opens automatically when exceeding set pressure; made using weights or springs |
| Pressure Gauge | Displays current pressure |
| Water Level Gauge | Shows how much water remains |
| Feedwater System | Allows water to be replenished even during operation |
| Firebox Cutoff | Reduces fire when water is insufficient |

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The most common type of accident occurs when water drops, exposing the tubes to steam so they overheat glowing red, and the moment cold water is replenished in that state, a massive volume of steam generates all at once and causes an explosion. Monitoring the water level is more important than tending the fire.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Efficiency also needs to be viewed soberly. The thermal efficiency of a small homemade steam engine is around 5% to 10%, meaning over 90% of the heat from burned firewood is wasted. Therefore, steam becomes a reasonable choice only when discarded heat can be co-utilized for space heating, cooking, or drying rather than solely for power generation.

### 4-3. Wood Gas Is a Battle Against Tar
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When wood is burned in an oxygen-deficient state, incomplete combustion produces a combustible gas mixture of carbon monoxide and hydrogen. Inside a gasifier, four zones form sequentially from top to bottom: drying, pyrolysis, combustion, and reduction; the core is the final reduction zone. Carbon dioxide generated in combustion passes through the glowing red charcoal bed below, converting back into carbon monoxide, which serves as fuel.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cooling and filtering this gas allows it to run an internal combustion gasoline engine. However, because the energy content of wood gas is much lower than gasoline, engine output drops to 40–50%.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In practice, what ruins this system is tar. If tar from the pyrolysis zone leaves mixed in the gas, it cools and sticks to engine intake valves and piston rings, rendering the engine unusable within days. Therefore, cyclone separators, coolers, and multi-stage filters are strictly required; building this purification system properly is harder than building the gasifier itself. To minimize tar, the structure must be designed so pyrolysis gas is forced through the high-temperature charcoal bed.

> **One of the main components of wood gas is carbon monoxide. Because it is colorless and odorless, there is no way to notice leaks, and humans will feel no abnormality until they collapse. Under no circumstances should it be operated indoors, in sheds, garages, or inside vehicles.**

### 4-4. Human Power Output Is Smaller Than You Think
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The sustainable mechanical output a healthy adult can maintain for over an hour without exhaustion is roughly 75–100W. Higher outputs can be sustained briefly, but will result in stoppage within minutes. Factoring in generator efficiency and wiring losses, actual electricity entering the battery is around 50–75W.

| Pedaling Time | Energy Yield | What Can Be Done |
|---:|---:|:---|
| 10 minutes | 8–13Wh | Fully charge a mobile phone once |
| 1 hour | 50–75Wh | Run a single LED bulb for over 10 hours |
| 8 hours/day | 400–600Wh | Barely run a single small refrigerator |

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The last line shows the reality of human power generation. Keeping a person pedaling all day long merely runs a single refrigerator, during which that person can perform no other work and must consume far more food than usual. Because food caloric input is far greater than electrical energy obtained, it cannot function as a continuous power source.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Nevertheless, human power generation is meaningful in specific scenarios: when a two-way radio must be turned on immediately, when overcast days have depleted batteries, or when other power generation equipment fails. As a last resort, it is worth maintaining because it is independent of weather and has few parts that can fail.
