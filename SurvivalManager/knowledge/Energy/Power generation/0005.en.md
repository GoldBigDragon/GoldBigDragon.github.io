## 4. Wind, Steam, Wood Gas, Human Power
### 4-1. Wind Power
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Wind power output is proportional to the cube of wind speed. At half the wind speed, output is 12.5%; at twice, it is 8 times.

| Wind speed | Relative output |
|---:|---:|
| 3 m/s | 1 |
| 4 m/s | 2.4 |
| 5 m/s | 4.6 |
| 6 m/s | 8 |
| 8 m/s | 19 |
| 10 m/s | 37 |

- Where the wind is weak, enlarging the blades does no good. If the annual mean wind speed is below 4 m/s, the effort put into building the equipment is hardly recovered. Most of inland Korea falls into this category, and meaningful wind is found only on coasts, ridgelines, and open highlands.
- Output is also proportional to the area the blades sweep (the square of the diameter). However, doubling the diameter also increases the force, weight, and vibration on the blades, which the structure cannot handle.
- The most effective improvement is raising the tower. Near the ground wind speed drops sharply because of friction, so raising it to at least twice the height of the surrounding obstacles multiplies the output several times.
- A safety shutdown device is essential. When rotation speed rises in a strong wind, centrifugal force grows with the square of the rotation speed, and the blades fly off at the root. Methods in use are folding the tail so that the rotor takes the wind at an angle, changing the blade pitch, and braking electrically by short-circuiting the generator output.
- The most dangerous moment is when the battery is full and the load disappears, so a dump load is essential.

### 4-2. Steam Engines
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When water becomes steam, its volume increases about 1,700 times. If this expansion is held back in a sealed vessel, it becomes pressure that pushes a piston or turns turbine blades. The same fact is also the source of danger. When a pressurized boiler bursts, the hot water inside turns to steam all at once and explodes. Even a small boiler releases enough energy to bring down a building.

> **Caution:** Never build a boiler without a safety valve. Without a device that opens by itself and releases steam when pressure exceeds the set value, it is a bomb that could go off at any time.

| Device | What it does |
|:---|:---|
| Safety valve | Opens by itself above the set pressure. Built with a weight or spring |
| Pressure gauge | Shows current pressure |
| Water level gauge | Shows the amount of water remaining |
| Feed device | Allows water to be added even during operation |
| Firebox damper | Reduces the fire when water runs low |

- The most common accident: when water runs low and the tubes are exposed to steam and overheated red-hot, topping up with cold water produces a huge amount of steam at once, and the boiler bursts. Watching the water level matters more than stoking the fire.
- Efficiency: the thermal efficiency of a hand-built small steam engine is 5-10%, and more than 90% of the heat of the firewood burned is thrown away. It is a reasonable choice only when the discarded heat can also be used for heating, cooking, and drying.

### 4-3. Wood Gas
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Burning wood with a shortage of oxygen produces a combustible gas that is a mixture of carbon monoxide and hydrogen. Inside a gasifier, four zones form from the top down: drying, pyrolysis, combustion, and reduction. The key is the reduction zone. The carbon dioxide produced in combustion passes through the glowing charcoal bed beneath it and turns back into carbon monoxide, and this becomes the fuel.

- If this gas is cooled, filtered, and fed to an internal combustion engine, a gasoline engine can be run. The heating value of the gas is far lower than that of gasoline, so the output of the same engine drops to 40-50%.
- Tar: if tar from the pyrolysis zone leaves with the gas, it cools and cakes onto the engine's intake valves and piston rings, making the engine unusable within days. A cyclone separator, a cooler, and several stages of filters are needed, and building this cleaning train is harder than building the gasifier itself. To reduce tar, design the structure so that the pyrolysis gas always passes through a hot charcoal bed.

> **Caution:** One of the main components of wood gas is carbon monoxide. It has no color or smell, so you cannot notice a leak, and you feel nothing wrong until you collapse. Never operate it indoors, in a storehouse, in a garage, or inside a vehicle under any circumstances.

### 4-4. Human Power
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The mechanical output a healthy adult can sustain for over an hour without tiring is about 75-100W. After generator efficiency and wiring loss, the electricity going into the battery is 50-75W. Even if you can briefly produce several times that, you stop within minutes.

| Time spent pedaling | Energy obtained | What it can do |
|---:|---:|:---|
| 10 minutes | 8-13Wh | Charge a mobile phone once |
| 1 hour | 50-75Wh | Run one LED bulb for more than 10 hours |
| 8 hours a day | 400-600Wh | Barely supports one small refrigerator |

- One person has to stay on the pedals all day to keep one refrigerator running, and during that time cannot do other work and must eat far more than usual. The food energy is far greater than the electricity obtained, so it does not work as a continuous source.
- Cases where it makes sense: when a radio must be turned on right now, when the battery is flat after several cloudy days, and when other generating equipment has broken down. It is independent of weather and has few parts that can fail, so it is worth having ready as a last resort.
