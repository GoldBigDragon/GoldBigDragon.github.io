## Introduction
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Except for gold, native copper, and meteoric iron, most metals lie buried as minerals combined with oxygen or sulfur. Smelting is the work of breaking this bond to strip off the oxygen, and what does it is hot carbon (carbon monoxide). The temperature of a furnace is set less by the kind of fuel than by how fast oxygen is supplied (the blast).

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This document covers identifying and collecting iron ore (bog iron and iron sand), charcoal, coke, and reduction reactions, bloomery ironmaking, furnace temperature and blast, bronze, brass, and steel alloys and heat treatment, and casting, crucibles, and metal toxicity.
