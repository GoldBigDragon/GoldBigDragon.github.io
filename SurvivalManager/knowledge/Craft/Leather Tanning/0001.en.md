## Introduction
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A skinned hide left wet rots within days, and a hide simply dried hardens like a wooden board. Tanning is the work of preventing both to make leather that is soft and long-lasting.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A hide hardens because the collagen fibers of the dermis lose water and stick to one another. Tanning is putting something between the fibers so that they cannot stick; only the material that fills the space differs by method, such as brains, tannins, oil, and alum.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This document covers the structure of hide, the preparation steps, brain, vegetable tannin, and alum tanning, staking and smoking, and tools and the work environment. Most failures come from rushing through the preparation steps or skipping a step.
