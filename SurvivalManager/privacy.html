[Survival Manager] Privacy Policy

1. Purpose and Categories of Personal Data Processing

  Survival Manager (hereinafter referred to as the 'Company' or 'Service') does not transmit, store, or share users' personal information, nor does it collect or use any personal information during the use of the Service. There is no membership registration process, and no personally identifiable data is transmitted to or stored on any server.

2. Device Access Permissions and Local Data Processing

  The Service may request the following device permissions to provide seamless functionality, but all data is stored and processed locally on the device and is never transmitted to external servers.
  • Camera: Scanning QR code ration tickets, etc.
  • Microphone: Morse code sound recognition and translation, etc.
  • Storage (Photos/Media/Files): Loading offline map images, data backup and restoration, etc.
  • Location: Displaying location in astronomy tools and offline maps, etc. (GPS data is calculated locally and not collected)
  • Bluetooth: Short-range group chat via Bluetooth, etc. (Messages are encrypted and visible only to the chat group)

3. Installation, Operation, and Refusal of Automatic Data Collection Devices

  The Service does not use 'cookies' that store and retrieve user usage information, nor does it collect any advertising identifiers (ADID/IDFA) for personalized advertising.

4. Third-Party Provision and Entrustment of Personal Information

  Since the Service does not collect any personal information, under no circumstances will personal information be provided to third parties or outsourced for external processing.

5. Rights, Duties, and Exercise Methods of Users and Legal Representatives

  As the Service does not collect or retain personal information, there is no subject matter for requests regarding viewing, correcting, deleting, or suspending processing. However, if you have general inquiries regarding the use of the Service, please contact the developer below.

6. Privacy Officer and Grievance Handling Department

  The developer has designated the following contact method to collect feedback and handle complaints from users.
  • Email: goldbigdragon@gmail.com

7. Changes to the Privacy Policy

  This Privacy Policy takes effect on September 1, 2026. Should it become necessary to collect personal information due to future service updates, we will clearly notify you and obtain your consent in advance in accordance with applicable laws.
